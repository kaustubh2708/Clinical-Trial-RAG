## 1. Study Overview
Protocol ID: NCT90000002
Title: A Phase 2, Open-Label Study of ONX-77 in Combination with Pembrolizumab in Previously Treated Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC).
Sponsor: Example Oncology Inc (fictional).
Primary objective: Objective response rate (ORR) per RECIST v1.1.

## 2. Study Design
Approximately 120 participants will receive ONX-77 400 mg orally once daily plus pembrolizumab 200 mg intravenously every 3 weeks until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria

Inclusion Criteria:
- Age 18 years or older.
- Histologically or cytologically confirmed stage IIIB or IV non-small cell lung cancer.
- Disease progression on or after one prior platinum-based chemotherapy regimen.
- ECOG performance status of 0 or 1.
- At least one measurable lesion per RECIST v1.1.
- Adequate organ function, defined as:
  a. Absolute neutrophil count of at least 1.5 x 10^9/L
  b. Platelets of at least 100 x 10^9/L
  c. Creatinine clearance of at least 50 mL/min

Exclusion Criteria:
- Known EGFR mutation or ALK rearrangement.
- Untreated or symptomatic central nervous system (CNS) metastases.
- Prior treatment with any anti-PD-1, anti-PD-L1, or anti-CTLA-4 antibody.
- Active autoimmune disease requiring systemic treatment within the past 2 years.
- History of interstitial lung disease or (non-infectious) pneumonitis requiring steroids.
- QTc interval greater than 470 ms on screening ECG.
- Receipt of chemotherapy or radiotherapy within 4 weeks before the first dose.

## 4. Study Treatment
ONX-77 is taken orally once daily with food. Strong CYP3A4 inhibitors must be avoided during treatment with ONX-77.

## 5. Safety Monitoring
Immune-related adverse events will be graded using CTCAE v5.0. Participants with grade 3 or higher pneumonitis must permanently discontinue both study drugs.
